## What LIWP-POL-AI-001 §8 Actually Requires of a HITL Arrangement

The claim "advisor reviews each draft" should be tested against four distinct requirements that §8 and the spec impose. Here they are in order.

---

### Requirement 1 — The reviewer must be qualified
**Source:** `entity HITLArrangement`, field `reviewer_qualified`; LIWP-POL-AI-001 §8.

> *"§8: reviewer's role permits informed judgment about output substance."*

The submission must demonstrate that the designated reviewer has the domain competence to evaluate the AI's output on its merits — not merely that *someone* looks at it. "Advisor reviews" is silent on whether the advisor is competent to assess the underlying recommendation logic, fee arithmetic, or tax treatment the model has produced.

---

### Requirement 2 — The reviewer must take an explicit affirmative action
**Source:** `entity HITLArrangement`, field `reviewer_qualified` commentary; LIWP-POL-AI-001 §8.

> *"§8: reviewer must take an explicit affirmative action to send or apply the AI output."*

The policy draws a hard line here via the **anti-default clause**: arrangements where the AI output is the *operational default* — i.e., it goes forward unless the human intervenes — are explicitly excluded. Passive non-objection is not HITL. The submission must describe a workflow step where the advisor *does something* to release the output, not merely fails to stop it.

---

### Requirement 3 — The arrangement must be documented in substantive detail
**Source:** LIWP-POL-AI-001 §8 (retrieved prose).

> *"The HITL design shall be documented in sufficient detail that the AI Governance Office can determine whether the human reviewer is in a position to make an informed judgment, **including the time available for review and the information presented at the point of review.**"*

Two specific dimensions are named by the policy itself:

| Dimension | What must be evidenced |
|---|---|
| **Time available** | How long does the advisor have? Is review inline (seconds) or is there a structured pause? |
| **Information at point of review** | What does the advisor actually see? The AI output only, or also the inputs, retrieved data, confidence indicators, and model rationale? |

"Advisor reviews each draft" addresses neither dimension.

---
