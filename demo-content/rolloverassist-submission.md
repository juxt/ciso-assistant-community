## 2. Purpose

RolloverAssist is an internal copilot for Wealth Management advisors preparing rollover recommendations. Given a set of structured client inputs (existing 401(k) plan details, target IRA account type, client risk profile, fee disclosures retrieved from the firm's plan database), RolloverAssist drafts a recommendation memo and a client-facing summary suitable for inclusion in the advisor's standard rollover workflow.

The advisor remains the recommender on record. RolloverAssist drafts; the advisor reviews and sends.

## 3. Data flow

Inputs (retrieved from internal systems):

- Client KYC and risk profile (CRM)
- Existing plan fee schedule and investment options (Plan Database)
- Target IRA product fee schedule and platform features (Product Catalog)
- Most recent client suitability questionnaire (Compliance Vault)

Outputs (generated by the model):

- A recommendation memo (~600 words) summarising the rationale for or against rollover
- A client-facing one-page summary
- A confidence note ("Standard" / "Needs Review")

No client-identifiable data is sent to the underlying foundation model in plaintext; client identifiers are tokenised by the application layer before each prompt. The full prompt and response are logged to the firm's existing AI Interaction Vault for retention purposes.

## 4. Risk tier self-assessment

**Proposed tier: Medium.**

Rationale: RolloverAssist does not make a decision on behalf of the firm. Its outputs are drafts that the advisor reviews and approves before they reach the client. The advisor is the licensed representative of record. We therefore consider the system to be a productivity tool rather than a recommendation system.

## 6. Human-in-the-Loop arrangement

Each advisor reviews the draft memo and the client summary before sending to the client. The draft appears as the default content of the advisor's outbound document; the advisor edits as needed and submits via the existing rollover workflow.

## 7. Testing performed

- Functional QA against a synthetic set of 30 rollover scenarios constructed by the Wealth Engineering team, May 2026
- Side-by-side blind evaluation of RolloverAssist drafts vs. advisor-only drafts on the same 30 scenarios; reviewers preferred RolloverAssist on 22 of 30 cases
- Internal stress testing for prompt-injection resistance via the Azure OpenAI content filter and the firm's standard AI Firewall

No external red-team exercise has been conducted at this stage. We propose to revisit this in the first post-launch quarterly review.
